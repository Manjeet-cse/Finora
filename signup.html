Frequently Asked Questions

How long does account creation take?

Your account will be created in just 5 minutes. You can start trading and investing immediately after completing your KYC verification.

What documents do I need?

You'll need your PAN, Aadhaar, and a bank account. We'll guide you through the process step by step.

Is there a minimum investment amount?

No minimum required! You can start investing with as little as ₹100 in mutual funds or ₹1 in stocks.

Do you charge account opening fees?

No! Account opening is completely free. You only pay charges when you trade or invest.
